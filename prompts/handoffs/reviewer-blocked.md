# Reviewer Blocked Handoff

```text
The latest review outcome is `blocked`.

Read:
- `AGENTS.md`
- `.concoct/current/task-plan.md`
- `.concoct/current/notes.md`
- the latest review
- relevant roadmap and capability context

Route the blocker to:

Product Owner when:
- product intent, capability behavior, scope, or priority is undecided.

Task Planner when:
- the plan is incomplete, contradictory, too broad, or not objectively reviewable.

Developer when:
- required code, tests, documentation, or evidence are missing within approved scope.

Human decision-maker when:
- authorization or external knowledge is required.

Produce a handoff with:
- blocker;
- evidence;
- why review cannot continue;
- required decision or work;
- responsible role;
- artifacts to update;
- recommended next action.

Do not mutate another role's owned artifacts until operating under that persona.
```
